|| OR (conditions):
1       2   |   Output
T       T   |   T

-----------------------

Priority        |       Operator
1               |       ! (not operator)
2               |       *, /, % (arithmetic operator)
3               |       +,- 
4               |       <, <=, >, >= (relational operator)
5               |       ==, != 
6               |       && {logical operator} (and)
7               |       || {logical operator} (or)
8               |       = (assignment)

AKA -> {NOT > ARITHMETIC > RELATIONAL > LOGICAL > ASSIGNMENT}

-----------------------

ASSIGNMENT OPERATOR 

= , += , -= , *= , /= , %=

if there is single line output for if-else statement we use ternary operator:

>> conditions ? result-if-true : result-if-false;
>> age >= 18 ? printf("adult \n") : printf("child \n");

(it is similar to):
if ( age >= 18 ){
    printf("adult \n");
} else {
    printf("child \n");
}
